What this is

A room behind the hardware store on Fifth with about two hundred tools in it. You borrow one for a week, you bring it back, you do not buy a tile saw you will use once.

Anyone who lives in the neighborhood can join, and joining is free — no income check, no proof of ownership, no deposit except on the four heavy items noted below. Bring something with your address on it the first time. Volunteers staff the desk; nobody here is paid.
